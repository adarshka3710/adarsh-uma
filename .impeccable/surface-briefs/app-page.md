# Anniversary experience surface brief

## Approval record

The user approved the “Cinematic Anniversary Journey” implementation plan in this Cursor session on 2 September 2026 and explicitly instructed the agent to implement it.

## Scope and visitor mode

An Experience-mode, single-page fifth-anniversary gift from Adarsh to Uma, primarily opened on a phone.

## Job and content

Uma should feel that Adarsh made a private place where their five years live. The story must move between romance, humor, nostalgia, long-distance tenderness, and hope. Until real private media is supplied, clearly labeled placeholders stand in for photos and factual memories.

## Chosen direction

“After-hours memory cinema”: midnight wine, blush dusk, aged champagne, film perforations, tactile photographs, and handwritten marginalia. A five-frame film device carries the timeline and becomes the story’s visual spine. The first viewport is a dark cinema title card with an intimate envelope action.

## Constraints

Mobile-first, optional user-initiated audio, local-only “Miss You,” no location permission, no copyrighted music, reduced-motion support, keyboard access, centralized editable content, and no generic heart-filled anniversary-template treatment.
