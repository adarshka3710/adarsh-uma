# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Uma is the primary visitor. She will most likely open the experience on her phone, privately, on 23 September. Adarsh is the editor and gift-giver.

## Product Purpose

This is a personal fifth-anniversary gift from Adarsh to Uma. It turns their five-year, currently long-distance relationship into an intimate place for memories, jokes, difficult chapters, affection, and future hopes.

## Positioning

The experience is a chronological, interactive memory cinema belonging only to Adarsh and Uma—not a reusable anniversary greeting or wedding template.

## Operating Context

The main journey is one long, mobile-first story opened from a shared link. Adarsh maintains its memories, photos, captions, letter, relationship facts, distance, and optional song in centralized content files and public assets.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page Next.js experience with responsive layouts and accessible interactions.
- Optional user-initiated audio only; no sound autoplay and no copyrighted default music.
- No location permission. Distance is manually configured.
- “Miss You” is an on-page playful response and does not send a real message.
- The first build uses clearly marked fictional placeholders until Adarsh supplies real memories and media.
- Motion remains tasteful, mobile-friendly, and reduced when the visitor requests reduced motion.

## Brand Commitments

Names: Adarsh and Uma. Anniversary: 23 September. Milestone: five years. Voice: intimate, conversational, romantic, funny, lightly self-aware, and never formal or template-like.

## Evidence on Hand

No real couple photos, locations, memory dates, private jokes, letter, or licensed audio have been supplied. Future work must not present placeholder incidents as factual.

## Product Principles

- Personal specificity matters more than generic romance.
- Humor and tenderness should alternate so the emotional moments earn their weight.
- The phone experience is the primary experience.
- Content editing should not require changing component code.
- Every interaction must remain understandable without motion or sound.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, semantic structure, touch-sized controls, dialog focus handling, and `prefers-reduced-motion` support are required.
